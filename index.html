FAQ

Questions we hear every week.

Can't find yours? Ask us directly and we'll answer within a working day.

Do I need to buy the machine?

Not necessarily. You can buy a machine outright, or host one at your site on a placement or revenue-share model where Miss Vendy owns and operates it. We'll recommend what suits your location.

Who refills and maintains the machine?

On managed placements, our team handles refills, cleaning and service using live stock data. If you own the machine, you can run operations yourself with our dashboard and support.

Which payment methods are supported?

UPI QR is the fastest path. Machines can also accept RuPay, Visa and Mastercard tap-to-pay, wallets, and closed-loop RFID or campus cards. Cash can be added where a site needs it.

Can the machine carry my brand?

Yes. Wraps, colours, screen content and product layout can all be customised for a brand, campus or corporate programme.

What about FSSAI and MRP rules?

Packaged food sold from a machine needs FSSAI registration and must follow Legal Metrology rules, including never selling above MRP. We help operators set this up correctly.
